## Dates, provenance and coverage

- **October 3, 2026: analyst-assessment cutoff.** Company policy judgments retain
  this date and their prospective horizon through October 3, 2027. Section A uses
  documents published by this cutoff. A later retrieval does not become an earlier
  publication or demonstrate that a researcher had the document on October 3.
- **October 31, 2026, 04:34:12.239389 UTC: scorecard-observation cutoff.** Section B
  includes late-October financial filings used in the retained scorecard. They must
  not be used to justify an October 3 policy judgment or an October 3 portfolio.
- **Retained input** means the document or expressly identified web rendering is
  listed in the retained r4 analyst records, and its saved object's SHA-256 matched
  that record. These records were assembled/recovered later in October; this label
  does not reconstruct earlier approval or collection history. Public links and
  section locators below were checked again for this supplement.
- **Recorded financial input / filing linked now** means the retained scorecard
  identifies the SEC accession and reported value through its company-facts record.
  The underlying filing is linked now for inspection; it is not misrepresented as
  a raw filing originally downloaded by that pipeline.
- **New corroboration** means a reference added for this supplement. It is not
  claimed as an original input, even if its publication predates the cutoff.

Coverage is **selective**: at least one primary document for every company, the main
PLUG financing documents, and selected financial inputs. It does not substantiate
every factor, award total, relationship, or assertion that no adverse event existed.
Publication dates below are filing dates or the issuer/agency's printed date, not
the financial period end. No publication time of day is inferred. SEC accessions
identify the filing; named sections locate the supporting passage within it.

[Form 10-Q, quarter ended June 30, 2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1274494/000127449426000170/fslr-20260630.htm),
First Solar; filed **July 30, 2026**; SEC **0001274494-26-000170**.

## Remaining gaps and limits

1. **Original support not recovered at the required claim level.** This review did
   not recover a complete document-to-value trail for every industrial-network
   relationship/count, lobbying and campaign aggregate, governance/ownership
   scalar, or every override in the published evaluation. A retained identifier
   or hash without the relevant dated passage and calculation is insufficient.
   Those inputs remain unsubstantiated by this supplement; no replacement source
   is silently promoted to an original input. Earlier approval lineage also remains
   unavailable, as the signed report already states.
2. **Bounded absence findings.** These selected affirmative documents do not reproduce
   the entire later-filing search or court docket for any company. They cannot prove
   that no adverse order, withdrawal or missed milestone existed anywhere before
   October 3, or that none would arise by October 3, 2027. Private milestone dates
   and cross-project triggers were not recovered from the public record.
3. **Award aggregation and valuation.** Not every jurisdiction/program or September
   award behind the retained registry totals is traced here. In particular A5 does
   not cover GFS's separate September R&D award. Conditional loan limits, grant
   ceilings, recognized credits and cash receipts are different quantities.
4. **Estimates and defaults remain estimates and defaults.** B1–B4 do not validate
   all financial inputs. TSM's native-currency basis must not be mixed with different
   annual reporting figures; GFS, MU and PLUG ROIC overrides and the common cost-of-capital
   assumption are not fully audited here. Neutral factors are not observed facts.
5. **Original representations and current links are different artifacts.** FSLR's
   July quarterly source, Micron's July release and the DOE notice were retained as
   web representations. Their saved hashes authenticate those representations,
   not raw issuer/SEC bytes. The links here reached the cited documents when checked;
   an accessible link does not reconstruct collection history or approval authority.
